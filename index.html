TechnoPiano

A piano-style hardware board powered by a BBC micro:bit for music, sensing, programming, and hardware control.

Open video in a new tab

Meet the programmable interactive robot

The piano board has touch-style keys labeled C, D, E, F, G, A, and B, plus RGB lighting, a buzzer, external connection ports, switch-board sockets, and interfaces for additional modules. It supports interactive projects involving sound, lights, and user controls.

The BBC micro:bit provides a 5x5 LED matrix, two buttons, a microphone, touch logo, light sensing, accelerometer, and magnetometer. Bluetooth, radio, USB, GPIO, analogue-digital pins, an edge connector, and external power support make it easy to connect computers, sensors, and other electronic components.
